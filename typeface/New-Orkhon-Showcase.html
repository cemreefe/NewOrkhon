About New Orkhon · sources and license

Typography, not a rewritten alphabet

Both main views use the same logical RTL text, including the inscription’s unwritten vowels. Only the letterforms change. New Orkhon’s capitals and lowercase are alternate glyphs, not Unicode case pairs. Mixed case capitalizes line starts, names and approximate sentence starts in the five complete Kül Tigin lines, and Latin capitals in the three modern samples. These choices are editorial, not features of the historical inscription. Modern samples are illustrative transliterations, not Old Turkic.

In the font, OpenType ss01 selects lowercase; disabling it selects capitals. The original script has no such case distinction.

Reference and limits

The reference is Noto Sans Old Turkic, not a facsimile or a claim about the exact shapes on the stone. Source Unicode strings are available under each passage. D1’s EZ variant and the display of word dividers are documented there. New Orkhon is speculative authored typography; reader performance and historical or linguistic validity have not been established.
